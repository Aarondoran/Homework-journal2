1. Overview

Homework Journal is a free application designed to help users organise homework, homework instructions, subjects, due dates, notes and timetable information.

Homework Journal is operated from Ireland. This Privacy Policy explains the information associated with the use of the application and how that information is handled.

By using Homework Journal, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Access and accounts

Homework Journal is currently available on an invite-only basis. Public account registration is not currently available.

Accounts are created on an as-needed basis. Users cannot currently create a Homework Journal account through a public registration page.

Access to the application requires an account. Users may sign in using the authentication methods made available to them.

3. Information collected

Account information

Homework Journal may process an email address for account and authentication purposes.

Homework Journal does not specifically require users to provide their real name. However, a user's name may be included within their email address.

Information you enter

The application stores information that you choose to enter. This may include:

  • Homework subjects
  • Homework tasks and instructions
  • Homework due dates
  • Notes
  • Timetable images

Information not intentionally collected

Homework Journal does not intentionally collect your precise location.

The application itself does not intentionally collect analytics or advertising tracking data, and Google Analytics is not enabled for the application.

4. How information is used

Information is used to provide the functionality of Homework Journal.

For example, homework information is used to display assignments and due dates, notes are displayed within the notes section, and timetable images are displayed within the timetable feature.

Email addresses may be used to identify and authenticate an authorised user.

Homework Journal does not sell user information or use the contents of homework, notes or timetable images for advertising purposes.

5. Data storage and security

Homework Journal uses Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide authentication and data storage functionality.

Information stored through these services may include account-related information and the information you enter into Homework Journal.

Firebase states that its services encrypt data in transit and that Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore support encryption of data at rest.

While reasonable measures and third-party security infrastructure are used to help protect information, no system, service or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for helping to protect access to your account and device.

Homework Journal relies on third-party services to provide parts of the application. These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Tracking, analytics and cookies

Homework Journal does not intentionally use advertising technology or Google Analytics. Analytics is not enabled for the application.

The application itself is not designed to perform additional tracking of users for advertising purposes.

Authentication services or other technical services used by Homework Journal may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies where necessary to provide authentication, maintain sessions or operate the service.

Third-party providers may process technical or service-related information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Sharing of information

Homework Journal does not provide a public platform for users to publish homework, notes or timetable information.

Users cannot use Homework Journal to share their homework or notes with other users through the application's own user-sharing features.

Information may be processed by the third-party service providers necessary to operate the application, including Firebase and GitHub.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security or integrity of the application.

9. Your information and account deletion

Downloading your information

Homework Journal provides a "Download My Information" feature. This feature is available to allow users to access and download their information without unnecessary delay.

Deleting your account

Users cannot currently delete their own accounts directly through the application.

To request account deletion, contact:

aarondoran@outlook.ie

When an account deletion request is processed, the associated Homework Journal data is intended to be deleted together with the account, subject to any technical limitations, backup retention periods or legal obligations that may apply.

10. Children and students

Homework Journal is designed as an organisational tool that may be useful to students.

Access is currently restricted to invited users and public account registration is not currently available.

Parents, guardians or users with questions about privacy may contact us using the contact information provided in this Privacy Policy.

Where applicable law provides additional privacy rights or protections to children or young people, those rights may apply.

11. Availability and important information

Homework Journal is a free application intended to assist with organisation. The application may experience interruptions, errors, maintenance, changes or periods of unavailability.

Homework Journal does not guarantee that the application will always be available, uninterrupted, error-free or completely secure.

Users should independently check important homework deadlines, school requirements and other important information.

Users should also retain backups or copies of important information where appropriate. The "Download My Information" feature is provided to help users access their information.

Additional terms concerning warranties, liability and use of Homework Journal may be provided in a separate Terms of Service. Nothing in this Privacy Policy is intended to remove rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Homework Journal, its data practices or applicable requirements.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed.

You should review this page periodically to stay informed about how information associated with Homework Journal is handled.
